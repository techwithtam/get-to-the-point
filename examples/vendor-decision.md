# Example: a vendor decision

This example is synthetic. The people, company, numbers and dates are invented.

## The request

> Get to the point on this draft. The reader is Dana, our operations director, and I need approval for the new scheduling vendor.

## The draft

> Hi Dana, I wanted to give you an update on the scheduling software evaluation. Over the past six weeks the team reviewed four vendors, ran two front-desk pilots and collected feedback. Northline was the only option that passed both pilots within budget. The cheaper option failed on rescheduling, which is the most frequent task at the desk. We had concerns about the data export, but the current vendor confirmed it can export our records during the April migration window, and we tested a sample in the second pilot. Priya will finish the contract review by Wednesday. If we don't have approval by Friday, March 6, we miss the April window and the current contract renews for another year at $18,000. If you approve, I will sign the order on Monday. Let me know your thoughts.

## The result

### Revised message

> Dana, please approve Northline as our scheduling system by Friday, March 6. It was the only option that passed both front-desk pilots within budget. Without approval this week, we miss the April migration window and renew the current contract for another year at $18,000.
>
> Priya finishes the contract review by Wednesday. Reply with your approval and I will sign the order on Monday.

### Prepared answers

- **What about the data export?** The current vendor confirmed it can export our records during the April window. We tested a sample in the second pilot.
- **Why not the cheaper option?** It failed the front-desk pilot on rescheduling, which is the most frequent task at the desk.
- **What if the contract review finds a problem?** Your draft does not say. Add your answer before you send this.

### What moved

- The six-week evaluation history is out of the message.
- The data export concern moved to the prepared answers.
- The draft's closing question became a specific next step.
